📚 Help & Documentation

Everything you need to know about using Temporal 3D QR Code system

🚀 Getting Started

For Teachers

  1. Open the Teacher Dashboard
  2. Enter session name and course ID
  3. Click "Start" to generate the temporal QR code
  4. Display the animated QR code to students
  5. Watch students check in automatically
  6. Click "Stop" when done and export attendance

For Students

  1. Open the Student Scanner on your phone
  2. Allow camera access when prompted
  3. Point your camera at the teacher's QR code
  4. Click "Start Scanning"
  5. Keep camera steady for 1-2 seconds
  6. Wait for confirmation message

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What makes this QR code "3D"?
The "3D" refers to using time as the third dimension. Unlike traditional 2D QR codes that are static images, our Temporal 3D QR codes change 60 times per second. This temporal dimension makes them impossible to counterfeit with screenshots or photos.
Why can't I just take a screenshot?
Screenshots are static images frozen in time. Our system requires capturing multiple sequential frames with progressive timestamps. When you try to scan a screenshot, all frames are identical, which immediately fails validation.
What if I don't have internet connection?
The system works perfectly offline! When you scan without internet, your phone generates a cryptographic proof of attendance that's stored locally and encrypted. Once you're back online, it automatically syncs to the server for validation.
How long does scanning take?
Scanning typically takes 300-500 milliseconds (less than half a second). The system captures 5-10 frames and validates them in real-time for the fastest possible attendance marking.
What devices are supported?
Any modern smartphone or tablet with a camera works! The system uses standard web technologies (HTML5, WebRTC) so there's no app to install. Just open the scanner page in your browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge all supported).
Can someone record the screen and replay it?
No! The system validates that timestamps are current (within 2-second window) and that cryptographic challenges haven't been used before. Old recordings are immediately detected and rejected as replay attacks.
What if scanning fails?
If scanning fails, try:
  • Moving closer or farther from the QR code
  • Ensuring good lighting
  • Holding your phone steady
  • Refreshing the page and trying again
If problems persist, check that your camera has permission to access the camera.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Camera Not Working

Problem: Camera doesn't start or shows black screen
Solution:
  • Check browser permissions (allow camera access)
  • Close other apps using the camera
  • Try a different browser
  • Restart your device

QR Code Not Detected

Problem: Scanner doesn't detect the QR code
Solution:
  • Ensure QR code is fully visible in the camera frame
  • Improve lighting conditions
  • Clean your camera lens
  • Try moving closer or farther
  • Make sure the teacher's screen is showing the animated QR

Offline Sync Not Working

Problem: Offline proofs not syncing when back online
Solution:
  • Check your internet connection
  • Open the student scanner page to trigger sync
  • Check browser console for errors
  • Clear browser cache and try again
